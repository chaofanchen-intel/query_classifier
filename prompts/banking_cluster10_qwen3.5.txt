A = card_payment_issues: a SPECIFIC payment going wrong — payment declined/pending/reverted, a payment or direct debit you do NOT recognise / did not make, virtual card issues, Apple/Google Pay. (A failed/unknown payment, NOT "where can I use my card".)

(Getting a card in a COUNTRY/region or where you operate → C, not E.)

J = identity_verification: verify identity, why verify, unable to verify.

Key distinctions:

- Wrong exchange rate on a CASH withdrawal or a CARD payment → F. Exchange rate / supported currencies in general → C.
- A charge you didn't make → A. A fee you're questioning → H.
- Getting a card / your service in a COUNTRY or region (US/EU), foreign cards accepted, where you operate → C. Which shops accept the card to PAY → E.

Output the single best-matching letter.
